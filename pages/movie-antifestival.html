Anti Festival


Antifestival (Athens, 2023)
Antifestival was a self-organized initiative held at the Kaisariani Shooting Range in Athens. It brought together dancers and musicians in an open dialogue between movement, music, and space. The event was based on a route: the audience started from one point and walked collectively to different stops, where performances and artistic collaborations unfolded. Almost every dance group collaborated with one or more musicians, creating live landscapes of sound and movement. The evening concluded with a small live rebetiko performance, giving the experience a sense of community and celebration.


Concept: Anthie Stasinou
Co-creation & Organization: Anthie Stasinou, Antzy Sarigeorgiou, Annamaria Karounou, Panagiota Bafouni, Olga Kalantzi, Fani Vakalopoulou, Irini Oikonomidou
