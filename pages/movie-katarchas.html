MOVIES ANTHIE STASINOU

Katarchas, Kalispera

A boy, Marios, gets into different taxis again and again. Each ride is short, ordinary, almost insignificant.

The taxi driver—an familiar, stereotypical figure—talks a lot: he asks, comments, assumes, categorizes.

Facing the stranger’s “neutral” gaze, Marios never tells the same story about himself. Sometimes he hides, sometimes he adapts, sometimes he performs a role that protects him. Only once does he tell the truth.

The film reflects on the violence of everyday conversation and the way social scrutiny forces people to invent identities in order to survive—especially when they inhabit bodies, genders, and desires that are judged before they even speak.
