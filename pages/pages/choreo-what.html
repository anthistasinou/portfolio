CHOREOGRAPHIC ANTHIE STASINOU

What I wanted, what I found

What I wanted, What I Found is a contemporary dance piece that reflects on the realities of an artist’s life in Greece—and beyond. Drawing from the performers’ own experiences, it contrasts the hopeful dreams of young dancers with the everyday struggles they later encounter: auditions, unstable work, service jobs, unpaid rehearsals, and the feeling of being treated as amateurs.

Through physicality, humor, and honesty, the piece exposes the gap between expectation and reality while celebrating the resilience of those who keep dancing anyway.
