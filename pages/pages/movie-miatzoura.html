Selected Projects ANTHIE STASINOU

MIA TZOURA

The film follows a group of young people through real conversations and moments from their everyday lives. They respond to questions about happiness, identity, work, dreams, and the need for connection. The conversations do not take the form of interviews, but unfold as spontaneous discussions between people who know each other.

At the same time, images of nights out, parties, dancing, laughter, and moments of intimacy are woven into the film. These moments become the space where the characters find time to share their thoughts and support one another.

One of the ideas running through the film is the relationship between identity and work. This transition connects the personal stories with the reality of work and adult life. The motif of smoking appears repeatedly throughout the film and is presented through stop motion. This technique briefly freezes time, creating small pauses between the conversations and the images of everyday life.

The film combines elements of documentary, fiction, video art, and stop motion, creating a cinematic portrait of a generation trying to find a balance between personal concerns and small moments of collective relief. Without offering definitive answers, it ends with images of people who continue to laugh, be together, and support one another
